Frequently Asked Questions

What is a data breach?

▼

A data breach is any security incident in which unauthorized parties access sensitive or confidential information, including personal data (Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, healthcare data) and corporate data (customer records, intellectual property, financial information).

The threat of this is obvious when it comes to passwords, as a hacker can use the password connected to your leaked account to hack into other accounts or even your email that use the same password. However, for emails, it only exposes private data connected to the account the email belonged to. From simple things like Name, Date of Birth, and Gender, to more sensitive information like your Geographic location, IP Address, or even your Home Address.

The data breach that this website records happened to a company called Leak "R" Us where hackers breached it's databases and released the account details of all accounts created for their online services.

What do I do if my password is in a data breach?

▼

It is advised you atleast switch that password on all accounts connected to your email address and especially of your email address itself if you use the same password for your email address. Additionally change that password from every account you have regardless of whether it's connected to your email or not.

It is generally a bad idea to use the same password for multiple different accounts, especially if an account has very sensitive information like your credit card details.

What do I do if my email is in a data breach?

▼

You can continue using the email address that has been leaked, but just know that a hacker that looks up your email can find out possibly sensitive information about you.

Depending on certain circumstances, this can be a non-issue. For example, if the data you have entered for the account connected to your Email address is fake or if you do not find the data leaked to be too exposing or accurate to your current circumstances, then you can choose to continue using that Email while being wary that any hacker that looks into you knows that information is connected to your email address.
