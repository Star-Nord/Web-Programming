KnowYourCyber

We are a charitable organization with the objective of increasing public awareness of cyber threats. We aim to help individuals protect themselves against cyber-attacks and how to react in an emergency.

You can use our services to search our data breach records to see if your Password or Email have been leaked. The leaks we index are from multiple dataleaks from the company Leak "R" Us when their servers were compromised in 2012 by hackers who leaked details of accounts registered for their online services.

You can also check out our Resources webpage to learn about Cybersecurity and get helpful tips on how to stay safe on the internet.
